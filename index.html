CampusParcel Dashboard

4 simple steps

1. Receive

Guard receives your parcel at the gate.

2. Register

Register an expected parcel and share your delivery OTP.

3. Secure

Guard stores it and emails you a pickup OTP.

4. Collect

Show the pickup OTP to the guard and collect it.

Know how this operates

CampusParcel is a web based application which you can use to collect your parcel in campus securely, without any hassle and without any chance of missing it.

If you order something online (Flipkart, Amazon, etc.) you can register the parcel on the "Register Your Parcel" page and type the delivery OTP your shopping app gave you. The guard sees it and uses it to take the parcel from the delivery person. Parcels coming from a friend or family member can be registered on the same page.

When the parcel reaches the gate, the guard stores it and a new pickup OTP is emailed to you. Show that OTP to the guard to collect your parcel. You can check everything on the "My Parcels" page.

Guards use the separate "Guard Login" page (PIN protected).

For any help, support or complaints you can go to the "Help & Support" section.
